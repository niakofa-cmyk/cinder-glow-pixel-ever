### 5. Code Optimization & Execution Protocol

Tips to get better results:
Build one module at a time (analysis first, then EQ, then compression, and so on), and test each with real audio.
Feed it reference tracks. Matching your mix against professional masters is how you get a measurable target.
Use your ears as the judge. No formula guarantees "the most harmonious sound." Mastering is partly subjective, so plan for adjustable presets and A/B comparison.
